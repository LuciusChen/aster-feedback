# Aster Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Last updated / 更新日期：2026-10-22

## English

Aster is a native Org workspace. You do not need an Aster account. Your Org files and adjacent attachments remain ordinary files, not records in a developer-operated cloud database.

### Files and optional services

Aster reads and edits the workspace you configure. Local indexes, previews, settings, drafts, and widget snapshots stay on your device or in the app's shared container. Cloud synchronization transfers files directly to the provider you choose: iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Nutstore, or your WebDAV server. Aster does not send a copy to an Aster server. Those providers process the transferred information under their own policies.

Optional Apple Calendar, Reminders, Photos, and Camera access is requested for the corresponding feature. Selected calendar information is read for display; enabled Reminders synchronization can read and update reminders. Photos and Camera provide attachments you choose to capture or attach. Direct Google Calendar access, when configured and connected, reads the selected calendars for display. Cloud credentials and integration tokens are stored using the platform's protected credential storage, not inside Org files.

Opening an external link or fetching its preview can contact the destination website. Your network address and the requested URL are then visible to that service. The website's own privacy policy applies. Aster does not execute Org Babel code.

### Purchases and diagnostics

Apple handles iOS/iPadOS subscriptions and payments. Aster uses Apple-verified transaction and subscription information on your device to determine editing access. Aster does not receive your payment-card details. Apple's privacy policy governs App Store purchases, system diagnostics, and TestFlight feedback.

Android subscription support is being prepared for 1.0.0 (34) and is not in the published 0.1 (30) build. For that upcoming release, Google Play handles purchases and payments. Aster uses Google Play Billing on your device to query purchases and acknowledge completed subscriptions. Google processes purchases under its own policies.

There is no developer-operated purchase-verification service or Aster account. Aster does not send your purchase token, Org files, attachments or cloud credentials to an Aster subscription server. Aster does not receive your payment-card details or Google password.

The device keeps an encrypted, authenticated purchase-token hash representing the last confirmed editing access in app-private storage excluded from backups, not a raw token or an inferred expiry date. Offline or failed checks retain that access. It may continue beyond actual expiry or refund until the next successful Google Play check updates it. Aster does not intentionally log raw purchase tokens or purchase responses.

Aster does not include advertising or cross-app tracking. It does not upload your workspace for developer analytics. If you email support or submit a GitHub issue, the information you voluntarily include is handled by the mail or issue-hosting service and used to respond to your request. Do not include passwords, tokens, or private workspace content in public issues.

### Your choices

You can revoke system permissions in device Settings and disconnect optional cloud services in Aster. Disconnecting and deleting local copies are separate choices. Subscription expiration does not delete or move your files. Files remain readable, including their source; creating or editing content in Aster requires an active subscription or trial. Copies held by your cloud provider are governed by that provider's retention and deletion controls.

For privacy questions or support, contact **chenyh572@gmail.com**. If you have sent support information and want it removed, contact the same address. Information needed to handle the request or meet legal obligations may need to be retained.

## 简体中文

Aster 是原生 Org 工作区，不要求注册 Aster 账号。Org 文件与同目录附件仍是普通文件，不会转换成开发者云端数据库中的记录。

### 文件与可选服务

Aster 读取和编辑你配置的工作区。本地索引、预览、设置、草稿和小组件快照保存在设备或应用共享容器中。云端同步会直接与所选服务交换文件，包括 iCloud Drive、Dropbox、坚果云或你指定的 WebDAV 服务器，不会另传一份到 Aster 服务器。这些服务如何处理数据，以各自的隐私政策为准。

Apple 日历、提醒事项、照片和相机权限仅用于对应功能。所选日历信息用于只读展示；启用提醒事项同步后，Aster 可以读取和更新提醒事项。照片和相机用于你主动选择或拍摄的附件。直接连接 Google 日历时，Aster 读取所选日历用于展示。云端凭据和集成令牌使用系统受保护的凭据存储，不写入 Org 文件。

打开外部链接或获取链接预览时，可能会连接目标网站。对方可以看到网络地址和请求的网址，数据处理遵循该网站的隐私政策。Aster 不执行 Org Babel 代码。

### 购买与诊断

iOS/iPadOS 订阅和付款由 Apple 处理。Aster 在设备上使用 Apple 验证的交易和订阅信息判断编辑权限，不会取得支付卡信息。App Store 购买、系统诊断和 TestFlight 反馈遵循 Apple 的隐私政策。

Android 订阅功能正在为 1.0.0 (34) 准备中，尚未包含在已发布的 0.1 (30) 中。后续版本的购买和付款由 Google Play 处理。Aster 在设备上通过 Google Play Billing 查询购买状态并确认已完成的订阅。Google 按自身政策处理购买信息。

Aster 不使用开发者运营的购买验证服务，也不需要 Aster 账号。Aster 不会向 Aster 订阅服务器发送购买令牌、Org 文件、附件或网盘凭据，也不会取得支付卡信息或 Google 密码。

设备会在不参与备份的应用私有存储中，保留经过加密和认证的购买令牌摘要，表示上次确认的编辑权限，不保存原始令牌或推算的到期日期。离线或检查失败时保留该权限，因此实际到期或退款后，权限可能延续到下次成功的 Google Play 检查再更新。Aster 不会主动记录原始购买令牌或购买响应。

Aster 不含广告或跨应用追踪，也不会为开发者分析上传工作区。如果你发送支持邮件或提交 GitHub issue，主动提供的信息会由邮件或 issue 托管服务处理，并用于回应请求。请不要在公开 issue 中附上密码、令牌或私人工作区内容。

### 你的选择

你可以在系统设置中撤销权限，在 Aster 中断开可选云服务。断开连接和删除本地副本是不同选择。订阅到期不会删除或移动文件，已有内容和源码仍可查看；在 Aster 中新增或编辑内容需要有效订阅或试用。云服务保留的副本由该服务的保留与删除机制管理。

隐私问题和使用支持请联系 **chenyh572@gmail.com**。如果希望删除曾发送的支持信息，也请联系此邮箱；处理请求或履行法律义务所必需的信息可能需要保留。
