# Aster Feedback Documentation Instructions

This repository is the public Chinese and English guide for Aster. Keep both languages aligned in meaning, while writing each one naturally for its readers.

## Writing Style

- Write for people, not as compressed storage.
- Prefer short, natural sentences and paragraphs of two to four sentences.
- Give each sentence one main idea.
- Separate behavior, rationale, consequences, exceptions, and procedures into distinct paragraphs or lists.
- Do not treat Markdown line wrapping as paragraph structure.
- Avoid repeating the same rule in adjacent sections. Link to the owning explanation when repetition would add no value.
- Keep Chinese and English semantically aligned, but do not force them to share awkward sentence or paragraph boundaries.

## Alignment

- Update the Chinese and English counterparts together when product behavior changes.
- Keep examples consistent with Aster's current PRD, specification, and changelog.
- Review the complete diff before committing. Remove stale statements instead of appending contradictory qualifications.
